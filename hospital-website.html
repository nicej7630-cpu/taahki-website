Comfort & amenities

Recovery deserves a good room, too

We treat hospitality as part of the treatment plan — because rest, good food, and calm surroundings measurably speed recovery.

Private rooms

Every inpatient stays in a single-occupancy room with a fold-out guest bed and garden or courtyard views.

Chef-prepared meals

A nutritionist-designed menu with restaurant-style room service, built around each patient's dietary needs.

Flexible visiting hours

Family can stay close — extended visiting windows and overnight accommodations for a designated caregiver.

On-site pharmacy

Prescriptions filled and delivered to your room before discharge, with follow-up refill support by phone.

Concierge support

A dedicated patient concierge to handle paperwork, insurance calls, transport, and family communication.

Free Wi-Fi & entertainment

In-room streaming, high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the campus, and a family lounge with games and reading.

Wellness & spa suite

Guided meditation, massage therapy, and a rooftop garden for patients well enough to step outside.

Free parking & shuttle

Covered parking for visitors and a free shuttle to the downtown transit hub every twenty minutes.
