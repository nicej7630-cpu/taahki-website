Why patients choose Taahki

Steady, straightforward care from people who explain things clearly

Taahki Health Center — جمعية اسرة التآخي — was founded in 1964 on a simple promise: every patient deserves a doctor who takes the time to explain what's happening and why. From your first visit, a care coordinator stays with you through diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up.

Our physicians come from leading training programs and choose to practice here because we protect the time they spend with each patient — no rushed ten-minute visits.
